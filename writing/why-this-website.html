Why I’m Starting This Website

October 20, 2026

Hi, I’m Naoki. For years, I thought LinkedIn and arXiv were enough to introduce myself. But I decided to make a website as a place to share my thoughts. Here, especially, I’m putting them out as they are.

Why?

Generative AI has become so commonplace that I feel it’s starting to contaminate not just the writing in the world, but my own thinking too. When I tell AI what I’m thinking, its feedback enters my head.

Everyone uses LLMs so casually now that this has become normal. But it means LLM-generated text is flowing into our thinking. Of course, there may be benefits for productivity. Still, I wonder whether the thoughts that emerge from the unproductive time we used to spend in college and graduate school are gradually being contaminated and disappearing.

I thought it might be worth having a place to record my own thoughts, before that happens, so someone—including me—can come back to them.

How I’ll Write

Mostly, I plan to talk through ideas in the car or while out for a walk and turn them into posts. Sometimes I might sit down at a desk and write, too. But I tend to put things off, so I want to make getting started as easy as possible.

I’ll probably use an agent to transcribe recordings and tidy them up a little. But the thoughts I write here won’t have gone through an interaction with AI.

I’ll probably be talking to myself in Japanese most of the time, but that should be fine. What I say will be translated into English here, and I don’t think it’s a big deal. I think your native language just comes out when you’re talking to yourself.

What I’ll Write About

I want to record my opinions on various topics. Sometimes—probably most of the time—they may not be useful. But perhaps they’ll offer a new perspective.

The aim is to exchange ideas with people.
