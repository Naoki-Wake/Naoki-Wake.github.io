SharedKV-BT: fast typed decisions for behavior-tree agents

October 19, 2026

This is an introduction to a paper I wrote recently. I’m still figuring out how to run experiments efficiently with agents, but for this paper I used them quite a bit for implementation and experiments.

The idea came from an earlier paper I wrote on behavior trees. I wondered whether I could combine that work with newer approaches to typed decisions, such as Jev. In thinking about how to design a framework that supports simple decisions, I wanted to explore what we could gain from using behavior trees (BTs), which are often used in robotics. I’m glad we were able to try it with several different kinds of agents.

The paper is linked here, and I think an LLM could explain its contents in detail if you asked, so I won’t go into the details here.
